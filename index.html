05 / Good to know

Before you
say hello.

Who can take part?

Executives, founders, professionals, coaches and other practitioners with relevant experience and something useful to share. You do not need to be a USJ alum. We’ll discuss the fit for the proposed session.

Is this a recruiting event?

Learning and dialogue come first. Relevant public opportunities can be discussed by agreement. No job or internship commitment is expected, and no hiring outcome is guaranteed.

Can we join remotely or bring colleagues?

Both can be discussed. Tell us whether you prefer a solo conversation, a panel or a team-led session. We’ll confirm what is feasible before announcing the event.

What about fees, travel and equipment?

Include any honorarium, travel, accessibility or technical requirements in your first message. Payment, reimbursement, equipment and venue arrangements must be agreed in advance; none should be assumed.

Will the session be recorded?

Photography, recording, quotes and publicity are discussed beforehand. Flag confidential topics, restrictions and any company review requirements during planning.
